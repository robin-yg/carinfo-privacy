CarInfo – Privacy Policy

Effective 14 October 2026 · Applies to the CarInfo Android app (package com.niborgy.carinfo)

In short: CarInfo does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal data. The app has no internet access, no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Everything it reads from your car stays on your phone.

What the app does

CarInfo connects to an OBD2 adapter (ELM327-compatible) in your car over Bluetooth. Through the adapter it reads fault codes, live engine values and vehicle information, and shows them on your phone and on your car's screen through Android Auto.

Data the app reads

  • Vehicle data from your car's diagnostic port: fault codes, freeze-frame data, readiness monitors, live values (speed, RPM, temperatures, battery voltage and similar), and the vehicle identification number (VIN) if your car provides one.
  • Adapter details: the name and Bluetooth address of the adapter you choose, so the app can reconnect to it.
  • Your settings, such as units, dashboard layout and cluster style.

All of this is kept only on your device, in the app's own storage. It is used only to show you the information in the app.

Vehicle reports and logs

When it connects, the app saves a vehicle report and a technical log of the adapter's replies on your device. These help with troubleshooting. They never leave your phone unless you tap Share or Copy, in which case they go only to the app or person you choose.

No internet, no tracking

  • The app does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere.
  • It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking libraries.
  • There are no user accounts and no sign-in.

Permissions

  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth): to find and connect to your OBD2 adapter. On Android 12 and newer the app declares that it never uses Bluetooth scanning to work out your location.
  • Location (Android 11 and older only): older Android versions require this permission for any Bluetooth scan. CarInfo never reads, stores or uses your location.
  • Android Auto: to draw the instrument cluster and show fault codes on your car's display. Android Auto itself is provided by Google under Google's own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling CarInfo, or clearing its storage in Android Settings → Apps → CarInfo → Storage, removes all data the app has saved, including reports, logs and settings.

Children

CarInfo is a tool for vehicle owners and is not directed at children.
